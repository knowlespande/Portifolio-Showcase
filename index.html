About Us

A school built around three things.

Academics. A full O-Level and A-Level curriculum taught by subject specialists, with dedicated study periods and exam preparation built into every term.

Character. Leadership programs, a student council, and community service requirements that carry through to Form 6.

Competition. Regional-level sport and a project culture that sends students to national science and robotics fairs most years.
